Last updated: 3 November 2026

Chertski Clip Bot captures highlights from a connected Twitch channel and publishes them to the connected TikTok account using official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Use

  • You may use the app only with Twitch and TikTok accounts you own or are authorized to manage.
  • Content posted through the app must comply with Twitch and TikTok rules.
  • The operator is responsible for the clips that are published.

Authorization

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, and Twitch Helix APIs, with OAuth. You may revoke access at any time in your Twitch or TikTok account settings.
