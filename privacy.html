Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Chertski Clip Bot is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the Twitch channel Chertski.

Data we process

  • Twitch OAuth tokens for the channel owner, used to create and download clips.
  • TikTok OAuth tokens for the same creator, used to upload videos to that TikTok account.
  • Clip metadata (title, clip id, publish status) stored locally on the operator’s computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not collect data from other TikTok or Twitch users.
  • We do not run ads or analytics on this site.

Storage

Tokens and clip history stay on the operator’s machine. They are not uploaded to this website.
