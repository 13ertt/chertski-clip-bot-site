Turn Twitch moments into TikTok videos.

Chertski Clip Bot watches a live Twitch stream, captures highlight clips, converts them to vertical 9:16, and publishes them to the creator’s TikTok with official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Open the app

1. Connect accounts

Sign in with Twitch and TikTok. We only request clips, chat, profile, and posting scopes that the product needs.

2. Capture highlights

The bot detects chat spikes and community clips, then downloads the official Twitch clip file.

3. Post to TikTok

Videos are rendered for TikTok and sent with Content Posting API (Direct Post or inbox draft).
